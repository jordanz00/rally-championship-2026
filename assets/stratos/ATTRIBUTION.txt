Lancia Stratos HF — playable chassis
------------------------------------
assets/stratos/stratos.glb — 1974 Lancia Stratos HF CAD GLB supplied for this
tribute (4 meshes, PBR albedo / MR / normal). Textures resized to 2048 for
browser boot. Not Sega's model.

Wheels ship fused per axle; js/cars/celica.js splits them into WHEEL_* hubs
at load so they spin and steer.

rival.glb — simplified LOD from the same hero (see tools/build-car-lods.sh).
stratos-loft-backup.glb — previous Blender loft hero.
stratos-lowpoly-backup.glb — earlier low-poly hero.
